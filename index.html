Privacy Policy for PixelFuse: Battle Arena

Effective date: July 20, 2026
Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bittechs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use PixelFuse: Battle Arena (“PixelFuse” or the “App”) on Android or iOS.

PixelFuse does not require your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone.

1. Information processed by PixelFuse

Account information

If you create an optional online account, we process your chosen username, password, account identifier, account creation time, acceptance of the terms, rating, tier, ranked-match count, and account status. Passwords are transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored only as a cryptographic hash. We cannot recover or display your password.

Your username, rating, tier, ranked-match count, and daily-challenge results may be displayed publicly in in-game leaderboards. Do not use your real name or other identifying information as your username.

Device and authentication information

PixelFuse processes a stable app or device identifier to authenticate online play, link an account to a device, prevent abuse, maintain sessions, and support account security. Account device bindings are stored on our server in hashed form. Authentication tokens and account session information may be stored locally on your device.

Gameplay and service information

When you use online features, we process information necessary to operate matches, including matchmaking mode, room participation, game inputs, match outcomes, rankings, ratings, daily-challenge completion time, defeated-enemy count, and related service events.

Our servers may also process standard technical information such as IP address, connection time, app build version, request status, security events, and diagnostic logs. We use this information to provide the service, diagnose failures, prevent cheating and abuse, and protect users.

Age group

PixelFuse asks for a birth year to configure age-appropriate advertising. The entered birth year is used on the device and is not stored by our server. The App stores only a derived audience group—child, teen, or adult—locally on the device. The App sends age-appropriate advertising treatment signals, not the entered birth year, to the advertising SDK.

Local settings

Game preferences, accessibility settings, audio settings, local progress, the derived audience group, and session information may be stored on your device. You can remove local data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to operating-system behavior.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

PixelFuse uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and user-selected rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to provide, measure, secure, and personalize or limit advertising as permitted.

Advertising requests are configured according to the derived audience group. Users identified as children or under the applicable age of consent receive restricted, age-appropriate advertising treatment. Eligible adult users may be shown a consent message and can review their advertising privacy choices from the App’s settings when required.

Google processes information according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. How we use information

We process information to:

  • Provide offline and online gameplay.
  • Create, authenticate, secure, and manage optional accounts.
  • Operate matchmaking, rooms, leaderboards, and daily challenges.
  • Maintain ratings, rankings, and game results.
  • Provide age-appropriate advertising and privacy choices.
  • Detect cheating, fraud, abuse, and service attacks.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability and performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable, we process information to perform our agreement with you, based on your consent, to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving PixelFuse. You may withdraw advertising consent through the privacy options shown in the App when available.

5. Sharing of information

We may share or make information available to:

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners for advertising, consent, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that operate the game service under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security of the service.

We do not sell your account information. Public leaderboard information is visible to other players as described above.

6. Data retention

Account information and associated game records are generally retained while your account remains active. We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy.

Some limited security logs, fraud-prevention records, and backups may be retained temporarily after deletion when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or service recovery. They are deleted or overwritten according to operational retention cycles and are not used to recreate a deleted account.

7. Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your PixelFuse account inside the App or request deletion by email. Never send us your password.

Delete inside the App

  1. Open PixelFuse.
  2. Open the Account screen.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Complete the displayed confirmation.

In-app deletion permanently removes the account and associated server-stored account, ranking, match-settlement, and daily-board data. This action is irreversible.

Request deletion without the App

Email bittechs.dev@gmail.com with the subject PixelFuse Account Deletion Request. Include your PixelFuse username, but never include your password. We may request reasonable information to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Account deletion does not automatically remove unrelated local settings stored only on your device. Clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App to remove local data, subject to operating-system behavior.

8. Children’s privacy

Online account creation is not intended for children who cannot legally consent to creating an account without permission from a parent or guardian. Such users should not create an online account without that permission.

PixelFuse uses the locally derived audience group to apply age-appropriate advertising restrictions. We do not intentionally use personalized advertising for users identified as children. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted personal information can contact us to request its deletion.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network connections, hashed passwords, hashed account-device bindings, access controls, and authenticated service requests. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence by us or our service providers. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international processing.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your information, or to withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise an applicable right. You may also review advertising privacy choices through the App when that option is available.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PixelFuse, applicable laws, or our service providers change. We will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or store listing when appropriate.
